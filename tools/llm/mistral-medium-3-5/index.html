Conflicts & caveats

  • Trae's built-in model menu may not include Mistral Medium 3.5 — Trae primarily ships with Claude/GPT/Gemini/DeepSeek.
  • Google Antigravity is built around Gemini 3 Pro; Claude and recent GPT-5 models also work, but Mistral Medium 3.5 is not in the supported model list.
  • Kiro's spec mode is tuned for Claude models — Mistral Medium 3.5 may work but spec-driven flows are best with Claude.
  • Cursor's built-in model menu may not include Mistral Medium 3.5 — verify it's available in your Cursor model picker.
  • OpenAI Codex (VS Code) is tied to OpenAI's hosted GPT-5 and Codex models — Mistral Medium 3.5 is not selectable inside this integration.
  • Gemini CLI is locked to Google Gemini models — your LLM is Mistral Medium 3.5.
  • Claude Code only works with Claude models — your LLM is Mistral Medium 3.5.
  • Devin uses Cognition's proprietary model internally — your selected Mistral Medium 3.5 will be ignored.
  • OpenAI Codex (Cloud) runs GPT-5.1 Codex internally — your selected Mistral Medium 3.5 will be ignored.
  • Jules runs Gemini models internally — your selected Mistral Medium 3.5 will be ignored.
  • The Claude Code SDK is tuned for Claude models — Mistral Medium 3.5 may work via the model adapter but loses Claude-specific features (thinking, prompt caching, sub-agents).
  • Lovable uses a fixed internal model — your selected Mistral Medium 3.5 will be ignored.
  • Replit Agent 3 uses its own model selection internally — your selected Mistral Medium 3.5 will be ignored.
